How will we learn which onboarding fixes work?

Long image

A

Answer

Turn each of the 13 friction points into a hypothesis that real patients can prove wrong. 10 get an A/B test, and 3 ship to everyone. Traffic is thin, so test whole new funnels first, then their parts.

  1. The portal gets about 197 sessions a day, so a 4-week test can only detect lifts of 3% to 14% relative.1
  2. The biggest drop, match to provider selected (36.6%), has a baseline today and is the first test to run (T-09).
  3. Six tests need tracking that doesn't exist yet: the care flow, payment success and the first visit.
  4. Safety screening and consent are never held back from a control group.

Every step is red: each one carries a friction point and at least one test.

  1. Entry: T-01 ↗

    H-01. "Tell us what's going on" vs. "Book Appointment Now". Measures visitors who finish intake. Needs marketing-site traffic.

  2. Account: T-02, T-03 ↗

    H-02. Account after the match vs. account first. H-03. Returning patients confirm what's on file vs. jump to providers. Need care-flow tracking.

  3. Coverage: T-04 ↗

    H-04. Member ID, live eligibility and a cost estimate vs. a logo pick. Guardrail: the insurance step (92.7% today) can't drop more than 2.6 points.

  4. Intake: T-06, T-08 ↗

    H-06. Plain-language conversation vs. diagnostic chips, 3 arms, detects 7% relative. H-08. Prefilled answers vs. asking again.

  5. Match: T-09, T-10 ↗

    H-09. Top 3 with a reason each vs. a long list; detects 8% relative on 63.4%. H-10. Dated slot, auto-booking waitlist or priced self-pay vs. "please call".

  6. Payment: T-12 ↗

    H-12. 2×2 of price breakdown × one-tap wallets. Needs a payment-success event.

  7. First visit: T-13 ↗

    H-13. A "Your first visit" card before booking vs. none. Measures show rate. Needs visit tracking.
