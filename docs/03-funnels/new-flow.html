The patient-first flow, and how much of it is built

The new funnel from the hypotheses and PRD v3: ten stages from "In your own words, what can we help with?" to a completed first visit. The prototype builds one path all the way through and stops every other path at its handoff. The reasons come after the flow.

What's prototyped, and what isn't

Built end to end

  • An insured Texas adult, first question to completed first visit
  • A self-pay Texas adult, the same distance
  • Every coverage failure, with its way forward
  • No openings and a taken slot
  • Closing the tab and coming back
  • Clinical questions refused and added to the summary
  • One clinician profile changing a match (the clinician side is that one screen)

Built to the handoff

  • Crisis route (clinician contact not sent)
  • Not a fit: other state, under 18 (no follow-up sent)
  • Talk to a person (no reply)
  • Waitlist, reminders, records request (nothing sent)
  • A/B control arms (each shows today's screen and stops)

Not built

  • Real insurer, scheduling, payment, email, text and health-record connections
  • A live model behind the navigator
  • Utah, booking for another adult, minors with a guardian
  • Rescheduling, cancelling, no-shows, follow-up visits, prescriptions
  • The care team's and clinicians' tools beyond the profile screen
  • Identity checks, prior authorization, coverage lookup without a member ID

Why it stops where it does

  1. The brief sets the finish line. Onboarding for a Texas patient ends at the first completed visit, so one path goes all the way there, for insurance and for self-pay.
  2. Depth goes where the thesis gets tested. The claim is that asking about the patient first, and making sign-up, coverage and matching work for them, lifts completed first visits. Those screens carry the A/B tests, so they're built in full, failures included.
  3. Handoffs stop at a person or a system Legion owns. The care team, clinicians, insurers and the crisis line sit behind these handoffs. Faking their side would hide open questions that need Legion's answers: who owns the safety rules, which eligibility vendor, the real self-pay price.
  4. Some paths are out of scope on purpose. Minors and booking for someone else are parked (D-02). Everything after the first visit is outside onboarding.
