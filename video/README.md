**Watch it: [https://youtu.be/Xg0KE33J9Ug](https://youtu.be/Xg0KE33J9Ug)** (about 4.5 minutes)

It covers the most important decisions and tradeoffs:

- What going through today's onboarding as a Texas patient showed, against the PostHog funnel Legion shared.

- Coverage before matching: one insurance screen, checked with consent, so every match is in network with a real price (test T-18).

On-screen notes cover what the narration doesn't: the safety gap, what makes it AI-native, what's built and what isn't, how we'll measure it, and how it was built. One detour (the under-18 path) is trimmed and labelled. The email typed during the demo is blurred.
